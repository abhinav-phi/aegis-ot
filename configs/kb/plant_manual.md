# SWaT Plant Manual (excerpt)

Stage 1 feeds raw water via pump P-101 with flow FIT-101 and level LIT-101.
Tank T-101 operating range is 0-100% with nominal 50%. Valves MV-201 isolate
stage 2. Flow meters read 0-15 m3/h. A closed valve implies near-zero flow.
Level change rate must not exceed 2% per second under normal operation.
All simulated actions in AEGIS-OT are labeled SIMULATED and never touch
real hardware.
